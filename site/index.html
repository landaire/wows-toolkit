Features

The toolkit reads the replays in your game folder and the game's own data files.

Replays

Replay inspector

Post-battle statistics for every player in a replay.

  • Damage dealt and received by type: AP, HE, SAP, fire, flooding, torpedoes
  • Spotting damage, potential damage, ribbons, and achievements
  • Builds, captain skills, and consumables for both teams
  • Damage between each pair of players
  • Chat log and match export
Ratings New

Battle impact

Battle impact (BI) rates a player's performance in one battle on the personal rating (PR) scale. Hover either rating to see its breakdown.

  • Shown in the replay table, search results, and stats
  • Stored in the replay index with the formula version
Renderer

Replay renderer

Animated minimap playback of a replay. The player below is the toolkit's renderer compiled to WebAssembly, playing three and a half minutes of a recorded battle. Change the layers, speed, or position while it plays.

  • Timeline scrubbing and playback speed
  • Detection, firing, and radar ranges per ship
  • Kill feed, chat, and roster overlays
  • Video export
Armor

Armor and model viewer

The 3D armor model of any ship, colored by thickness with the game's color scale. Any other model in the game can be opened too, including armored buildings and gun mounts.

  • Shell trajectory through each plate, with overmatch, ricochet, and penetration results
  • Penetration checker
  • Incoming fire from a replay plotted on the hull
  • Camouflage rendering and GLB export

The model at the top of this page has an armor view with sample values.

Stats

Session stats

Win rate, personal rating, damage, and other statistics across a session or a folder of replays.

  • Filters for division, game mode, and number of games
  • Per-ship table
  • Charts for any statistic
Players Redesigned

Current match and player tracker

During a battle, the current match view lists every player with their win rate, PR, average damage, and battles, for the whole account or for the ship they are in.

  • Team averages for win rate and PR
  • How often you have met each player, and your notes on them
  • Players and clans you have met over a chosen time period
  • Marks players who may be watching your Twitch stream

Search

Search all indexed battles with a query language, for example outcome:win map:ocean self.damage>=150000.

Unpacker

Browse and extract files from the game's packed archives.

Launcher New

Sign in to Wargaming Game Center, download and install game builds, and start the game. Supports Proton on Linux.

Tactics board

Place ships, capture points, and drawings on any map. A browser version is also available.

Listing previews

Hover a replay in the listing to see a minimap preview.

Command palette

Ctrl+K opens a command palette for navigation, replays, players, and settings.

Changes since 1.0

These are in the 1.1 beta releases.

01

New interface

A new desktop interface with panels that dock and split, and light and dark themes.

02

Launcher

Sign in to your Wargaming account, update or install the game, and launch it, with safe mode and Proton on Linux.

03

Battle impact

A per-battle rating on the PR scale, shown beside PR in replays, search, and stats. Hover either rating for its breakdown.

04

Model viewer

Open any model in the game, including armored buildings and gun mounts, and export it to GLB with its textures.

05

Current match and player tracker

Redesigned. Current match, players, and clans are panels you can place side by side, and your filters are kept.

06

Anonymized names

Replace every player name and clan tag with a generated one before you share a screenshot or stream.
